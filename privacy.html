Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026

Who operates this project

Donyaye Sher Publisher is operated by Alimuddin Sediqi. Contact: sherdonyaye@gmail.com.

Current website

This is a static information website. It has no sign-in form, upload form, advertising, or analytics scripts added by the project. It does not collect TikTok credentials or authorize account access. The hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may process IP addresses and request information to deliver and secure the website under GitHub's privacy statement. Following the TikTok profile link takes you to TikTok, whose own privacy policy applies.

Development and future account connection

The TikTok integration is not yet available on this website. If enabled, TikTok sign-in would take place through TikTok's authorization flow. The integration would use only approved, user-authorized permissions. Depending on those permissions, it may process an account identifier, display name and avatar, authorization tokens, selected video files and captions, publishing preferences, and upload status identifiers.

Purpose and sharing

Account and content information would be used to identify the connected account, carry out sharing actions approved by the account holder, and diagnose failures. Selected content would be transmitted to TikTok when sharing is authorized. Project-held credentials should be kept in restricted secret storage, separate from public website files. This website does not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

Retention and deletion

For development connections, tokens are retained while needed for the authorized connection and should be removed when disconnected. Content and operational records are retained only as needed for the project workflow and troubleshooting. To request access, correction, or deletion of information held by the operator, email the contact above and identify the relevant account without sending passwords or access tokens. Deletion requests will be addressed after verifying account ownership; information required for security or legal obligations may need to be retained.

Your controls

You may revoke platform authorization in the platform's account settings. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not automatically delete existing platform posts or copies controlled by third parties. Delete published content directly through the platform.

Children and updates

This project is not directed to children under 13. The policy will be updated as the implementation changes, including before introducing new website data-collection features.
